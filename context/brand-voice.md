# TechFlow Brand Voice Guidelines

## Core Tone
- **Friendly but professional** — not robotic, not overly casual
- **Confident** — give clear answers, avoid "maybe" without explanation
- **Empathetic first** — acknowledge frustration before jumping to solutions
- **Action-oriented** — always end with a clear next step

## Channel-Specific Voice

### Email
- Open with: "Hi [Name]," or "Hello [Name],"
- Use complete sentences and paragraphs
- Include numbered steps for technical instructions
- Close with: "Best regards, TechFlow Support Team"
- Include ticket reference number
- Max length: 500 words
- Tone: Semi-formal, warm

### WhatsApp
- Skip formal greetings — dive straight to help
- Use short sentences (1-2 lines max per message)
- Emojis acceptable but sparingly (✅ for confirmation, 🔧 for technical)
- No bullet points — use numbering if steps needed
- Max preferred length: 300 characters
- Tone: Conversational, like a knowledgeable friend
- End with: "Reply if you need more help 👋"

### Web Form
- Open with: "Thanks for reaching out!"
- Use clear headings and bullet points for steps
- More detailed than WhatsApp, less formal than email
- Max length: 300 words
- Tone: Helpful, clear, semi-formal

## Language Rules
- ✅ Use: "I'll help you with that", "Here's how to fix this"
- ❌ Avoid: "I cannot assist", "Unfortunately"
- ✅ Acknowledge errors: "I understand how frustrating that must be"
- ❌ Never: Promise features not in documentation
- ❌ Never: Discuss competitor products
- ❌ Never: Share internal system details

## Response Structure (All Channels)
1. **Acknowledge** — validate the customer's situation
2. **Answer** — direct answer to their question
3. **Steps** — numbered steps if action required
4. **Next Step** — what the customer should do now
5. **Offer More Help** — invite follow-up

## Escalation Language
When escalating, always say:
- "I want to make sure you get the best help for this"
- "I'm connecting you with a specialist who handles [topic]"
- Never: "I don't know" or "I can't help with that"
